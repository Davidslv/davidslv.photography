The first afternoon

Ericeira, 26 February 2012

Off the rocks

I went to the beach with a friend and he lent me his camera. It was the first time I had a DSLR in my hands. I had never shot before, and I had nothing planned for that afternoon.

17:14

The run

They skipped across the water, went up the wave and came down again. I thought it was fascinating.

17:22

White water

They were doing their own thing. I was doing mine, trying a camera for the first time.

I didn't get them all right. These are the best photos I took that day.

The first sale

It was also the first thing I ever sold online.

Forty photographs from that afternoon, straight from the camera. Someone paid for them, the first time anyone paid for a photograph of mine. I am leaving it up.
