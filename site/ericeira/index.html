The first afternoon

Ericeira, 26 February 2012

Off the rocks

I had a DSLR in my hands for the first time. I pointed it at people skimboarding off the rocks and I did not really know what I was doing.

17:13

The run

Run, drop the board, jump on. Again and again.

17:22

White water

Some are sharp. Some are not. That was the afternoon.

I came home with more than 3,000 photos. These are some of the ones I kept.

The first sale

It was also the first thing I ever sold online.

Forty photographs from that afternoon. Rocks, white water, wetsuits and boards. Someone paid for them, the first time anyone paid for a photograph of mine. I am leaving it up.
