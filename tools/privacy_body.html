Your details

What I keep about you, why, for how long, and how to ask me to change or delete it. In plain words.

Who I am

David Silva Photography, a trading name of CARERNOTES LTD, registered in England and Wales, company number 16928805. Registered office: 1st Floor 87/89 High Street, Hoddesdon, Herts, EN11 8TL. I decide how your details are used. For anything on this page, email hello@davidslv.photography.

If you email me about work

I keep your name, email, business details and what you tell me, so I can reply, prepare our call and send you a quote. If we don't work together, I delete them two years after we last spoke.

If you're a client

I keep your contact and billing details, our agreement, quotes and invoices, so I can do the work and keep my accounts. Company records are kept for six years, as the law requires.

The photos and videos I make for you, including of your staff, are kept in my archive for at least two years. They're only shown in my portfolio or on social media if you said yes to that in our agreement, and you can change that at any time.

If I look after your website, email list or social media, any of your customers' details I see stay yours. I only use them to do what you've asked, and I hand everything back when we finish.

If you're in one of my photographs

I photograph people at car meets, at events and in the street. If you'd like a photograph of you taken down from my website or social media, email me with a link to it and I'll remove it. I don't publish children's faces.

If you buy something

For the hardcover book, the terms of sale explain what's collected and who it's shared with. Payments are handled by Stripe, and I never see your card details. Digital products are sold on Gumroad, under Gumroad's own terms.

If you join my email list

I keep your name and email to send you what you signed up for. The list runs on Sender. Every email has a link to unsubscribe, and unsubscribing stops them straight away.

If you message me on Instagram

When you comment a keyword such as BOOK, an automatic reply is sent through ManyChat. Instagram and Meta's own privacy terms apply to messages there.

This website

To see how the site is used, I count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics and my own anonymous reading statistics: which pages are opened, how far they're scrolled, which photographs are on screen and for how long, and which buttons are pressed, with the type of device and the country. Neither uses cookies or stores anything on your device, and no IP address or other personal detail is kept. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, my statistics don't count you at all.

Who else handles your details

Only the services I need to run the business: Google Workspace (email and documents), Cloudflare (website statistics and the book shop), GitHub (hosts this website), Sender (email list), Stripe (payments), Prodigi (printing and posting books), Gumroad (digital products) and ManyChat (Instagram replies). I don't sell your details to anyone.

Your rights

You can ask me to see, correct or delete what I hold about you, to stop using it, or to send it to you. Email me and I'll reply within a month. If you're not happy with my answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at https://ico.org.uk.
