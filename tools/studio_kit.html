Questions

Straight answers.

I'm not technical. Is it for me?

You need to copy, paste and click Approve. Claude does the code, the DNS records and the checks, and tells you exactly where to click for the rest.

Do I need to code?

No. The step-by-step instructions are written for Claude. You never have to read them.

What if Claude gets something wrong?

It asks before every change, and nothing goes public, costs money or gets sent without your yes. Each module lists known problems and their fixes. Passwords never go in the kit folder.

Mac only?

It's tested on a Mac. The Windows steps are written but untested, and the kit says so.

I'm not a photographer.

It was built by a photographer for any solo creative: illustration, music, ceramics, writing, video. The interview adapts to your work.

Is it a course?

No videos to sit through. It's a folder of guides that Claude follows with you, one module at a time, until each part is live.
