1. What it is

A folder of guides for Claude Code, Anthropic's assistant that works on your computer. You open the folder, say hello, and Claude walks you through the business side of your photography, one part at a time.

  • Claude does the technical parts: the files, the DNS records, the website code, the image processing, the checks.
  • You do the parts only you can do: sign-ups, payments and approvals. Claude tells you exactly what to click.
  • Nothing is bought, published or sent without your yes.

2. What's inside

16 modules. Each has a plain-English guide for you and step-by-step instructions for Claude.

  • Your name online: domain, business email, brand, website, search and Google.
  • Reaching people: mailing list, social posting for Instagram and YouTube, replies to messages.
  • Getting paid: payments, selling downloads, print on demand, quotes and invoices.
  • Keeping it going: a weekly routine, and optional Notion, Gmail and Google Drive set-ups.

Also in the folder: a starting interview where Claude asks about your work and suggests an order, a roadmap of what depends on what, a website starter for a fast portfolio site in your own brand, and a printable 30 and 60 day plan built from your own numbers.

The free guides on this site are pieces of it: a business email on your own domain and a portfolio website for £0 a month.

3. How it works

  1. Unzip it into a folder on your Mac.
  2. Open Claude Code in that folder.
  3. Say hello. Claude runs the interview and starts with the first module you choose.

4. What it costs to run

The kit doesn't replace the services, so here is what you'd pay for them (October 2026; prices change):

  • Claude Pro or Max subscription, to run Claude Code.
  • A domain: about £10 to £40 a year.
  • Website hosting: £0 on GitHub Pages.
  • Email on your domain: Google Workspace is recommended, from £5.90 a user a month plus VAT.
  • Only if you choose those modules: social scheduling, automatic DMs, print and card costs per order. Stripe and Gumroad take a fee on each sale.
  • Your time: about 12 to 20 hours for the basics, at your own pace.

5. Honest limits

  • Tested by one person, me, on a Mac, in October 2026. Not yet used by outside buyers.
  • Some modules are only partly tested. The roadmap in the kit says which, step by step.
  • Mac first. The Windows steps are written but untested.
  • Written for the UK first. For tax and consumer law elsewhere, Claude checks your local rules on official sites.
  • It doesn't take your photos for you, and it doesn't promise sales, bookings or followers.
